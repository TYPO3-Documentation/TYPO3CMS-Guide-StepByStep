# Preview unpublished content in TYPO3

<!-- #TYPO3v13 #Beginner #Backend #Editing @dhubert974 -->

Previewing unpublished content in TYPO3 allows editors to review changes before they become visible to website visitors. This helps ensure content is accurate, correctly formatted, and placed in the right location before publication.

Learning how to preview unpublished content helps you validate updates and collaborate with reviewers without publishing incomplete work.

## Learning objective

In this step-by-step guide, you will learn how to preview unpublished content in TYPO3 and verify changes before publication.

## Prerequisites

### Tools and technology

* Access to a TYPO3 backend account
* Permission to edit content in TYPO3
* A TYPO3 website with unpublished or hidden content

### Knowledge and skills

* Basic familiarity with navigating the TYPO3 backend
* Understanding of page and content editing in TYPO3

## Preview unpublished content

Before publishing content, it is good practice to preview the page to ensure everything appears as expected.

1. Open the **Layout module** in the TYPO3 backend.
2. Navigate to the page containing the unpublished content.
3. Verify that your content has been saved.

   Hidden or scheduled content must exist on the page before it can be previewed.

4. Click the **View webpage** button in the top toolbar.

   A preview of the page opens in a new browser tab.

5. Review the unpublished content.

   Confirm that:

   * The content appears in the correct location
   * Images and links display correctly
   * Formatting looks as expected
   * Scheduled or hidden elements appear properly

6. Return to TYPO3 and make any necessary adjustments.

   Refresh the preview page after saving your updates.

## Troubleshoot missing unpublished content

If unpublished content does not appear in preview mode:

1. Confirm that you are logged into TYPO3.

   Some unpublished content is only visible to authenticated backend users.

2. Verify that the content is saved.
3. Check whether the content is hidden, scheduled, or restricted by permissions.
4. Refresh the preview page after making changes.
